Platform guide / PlayStation

DIALED IN.

A practical starting point for Warzone on PlayStation. Tune your controller, display and visibility around your own comfort and screen rather than copying settings blindly.

ALL SETTINGSCONTROLLER GUIDE
01 / Controller

Build consistent aim

  • Start from a sensitivity you can control while tracking a moving target.
  • Test horizontal and vertical aim separately before changing both.
  • Choose a response curve that feels predictable, then give it time before adjusting.
  • Keep deadzones as low as practical without unwanted stick drift.
02 / Display

Calibrate the screen

  • Use your display's Game Mode where available.
  • Calibrate brightness and HDR using the game's own calibration screens.
  • Enable higher refresh output only when both your console model and display support it.
  • Prefer stable frame pacing and clear visibility over effects that make targets harder to see.
03 / Field of view

Balance awareness and clarity

  • Increase FOV gradually and test at your normal viewing distance.
  • Very high FOV can make distant targets appear smaller.
  • Choose a setting that keeps movement comfortable and targets readable.
04 / Consistency

Change one thing at a time

  • Save a baseline before experimenting.
  • Test in the same mode and similar situations.
  • Review your own clips to spot over-correction or visibility issues.
  • Recheck settings after major game updates.

Game options and performance support can change between updates and console models. Treat this as a setup framework, then confirm the current in-game options and your display's supported modes.
