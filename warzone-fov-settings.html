FOV SETTINGS.

A practical starting guide for Warzone players who want a wider view without making targets unnecessarily difficult to read.

Starting range

Try a FOV around 100–110 and adjust from there. Higher FOV can show more of the surroundings but can also make distant targets appear smaller.

Test it properly

  • Play several matches before changing it again.
  • Test close-range fights and longer sightlines.
  • Keep the setting that gives you the best balance of awareness and target visibility.

FOV is personal. Display size, distance from the screen, performance and your own vision all matter.
