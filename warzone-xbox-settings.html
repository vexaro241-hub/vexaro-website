XBOX WARZONE SETTINGS.

Use these as simple starting points for a clean, responsive Warzone setup on Xbox.

Controller

  • Start sensitivity: 6/6
  • Response curve: Dynamic
  • Auto Sprint: On
  • Keep deadzones low enough for responsiveness without causing drift.

Graphics

  • Motion blur: Off
  • Use 120Hz when your Xbox and display support it.
  • Start around 100–110 FOV and tune to your screen.
  • Prioritise clear visibility and stable performance.

Settings should be tested in real matches. Updates can change menus and available options, so treat this page as a baseline rather than a fixed meta.
