MOVEMENT SETTINGS.

Use this as a starting point for smoother Warzone movement on controller. Tune each setting to your own playstyle.

Start here

  • Auto Sprint: On
  • Slide/Dive behaviour: choose the option that feels fastest and most consistent to you
  • Controller sensitivity: start around 6/6, then adjust
  • Response curve: Dynamic is a strong starting point
  • Keep your deadzones as low as your controller allows without stick drift

Movement checklist

  • Practise slide → sprint transitions.
  • Use jump and slide together with aim discipline rather than constant spam.
  • Learn your favourite routes and cover positions.
  • Record your matches and review movement that actually wins fights.

These are starting points, not universal best settings. Your controller, aim style and current Warzone update can change what feels best.
